Dr. Qi Li is the Cullen and McFadden Professor of Economics at Texas A&amp;M University. He received his Ph.D. in Economics from Texas A&amp;M University in 1991 and began his academic career that same year as an Assistant Professor at the University of Guelph in Canada. He advanced rapidly through the academic ranks and was promoted to Full Professor in 1996. In 1999, he joined the faculty of the Department of Economics at Texas A&amp;M University.

Professor Li is a theoretical econometrician specializing in nonparametric econometrics. His widely cited book, *Nonparametric Econometrics: Theory and Practice*, coauthored with Jeffrey Racine, has become a standard reference for researchers in the field. He has published more than 150 articles in refereed journals.

Professor Li is internationally recognized for several lines of research that have significantly influenced the development of nonparametric econometrics. With Yanqin Fan, he pioneered consistent specification tests for semiparametric models against nonparametric alternatives. With Peter Hall and Jeffrey Racine, he made a fundamental contribution by extending nonparametric smoothing methods to settings involving both categorical and continuous variables. In addition, with Li Gan, he was the first to derive a matching function from microeconomic foundations.

Professor Li has directed or co-directed 30 completed doctoral dissertations, and his former students hold positions at leading universities in the United States and abroad. He is a Fellow of the *Journal of Econometrics* and has received multiple research grants from both the Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council of Canada and the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada. In 1999, he was a recipient of the inaugural Ontario Premier’s Research Excellence Award. He was also ranked among the 20 most prolific authors worldwide based on the number of articles published in the *Journal of Econometrics* between 1973 and 2012.
